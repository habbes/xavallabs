Current product focus: Okapi — AI-assisted 2D animation software built for animator flow, speed, and creative control.

Current focus: Okapi

AI-assisted 2D animation is advancing quickly, but major gaps remain. We are working on the hardest practical problems for professional and independent animators.

  • Real-time, automatic in-between generation based on animator guides.
  • Models that adapt to an animator's style while they work.
  • Automatic colorization and lighting support.
  • Low-resource, efficient models that run on-device.

Open problems we are tackling

  • Existing systems are often slow and need expensive hardware, which breaks real-time workflow on consumer devices.
  • Many modern models depend on huge proprietary datasets.
  • Style generalization remains weak outside represented training styles.
  • Outputs can be unpredictable and limit creative control for animators with clear intent.

Research intent

Our objective is full creative control at the stroke level, immediate feedback that does not interrupt the craft, and tools that help animators move faster without getting in the way.
