Open problems we are tackling

01. INFERENCE SPEED

Reducing latency for real-time creative interactions without compromising fidelity.

02. DATASET DEPENDENCY

Moving beyond fixed, pre-trained datasets toward real-time learning that adapts to an artist's unique style and creativity without retraining.

03. STYLE GENERALIZATION

Ensuring models don't homogenize art but celebrate the diversity of human aesthetics.

04. CREATIVE CONTROL

Mapping high-level intent to low-level stroke data with surgical precision.
